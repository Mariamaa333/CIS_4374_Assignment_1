I said:
so when creating a WBS I pay more attention on deliverable of what the result/completion instead of a task( step by step)?

ChatGPT said:
Exactly. A WBS focuses primarily on deliverables/results, not on a step-by-step list of tasks.
Think of it as:

“What needs to exist/be completed?” rather than “What steps do I take to do it?”
For example, for your parking system:
WBS/deliverable-oriented:

Parking Reservation System
Reservation Interface
Space Availability Display
Reservation Confirmation
Reservation Cancellation
These describe things/components that will be delivered.
More task-oriented:

Open the database
Write the reservation code
Test the reservation button
Fix errors
Deploy the system
Those are activities/tasks used to produce the deliverables.
A simple way to tell the difference
Ask yourself:
“Can I hand this to someone and say, ‘This is what we're delivering’?”

If yes → probably a good WBS element.

“Is this an action someone performs to create it?”

If yes → that's more likely a task/activity.

So your original components are actually pretty good:

Driver Authentication and Security → deliverable/component
Parking Availability and Reservation → deliverable/component
Payment System → deliverable/component
Digital Reports and Analytics → deliverable/component
Then you can keep breaking those deliverables into smaller deliverables until you reach the required depth.
